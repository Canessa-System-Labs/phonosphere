<a name="the-idea"></a>
<img src="assets/ch-the-idea.svg" width="100%" alt="01 The idea: Speech synthesis and prosody studio">

Phonosphere Studio treats speech synthesis as a performance to direct, not a text box to fill. In the solo studio you choose a voice archetype, set its style and emotional intensity, tune pitch and speed, and mark breaths, laughs and pauses where they belong. In the screenplay studio you paste a full two-person script; each line is attributed to its speaker and the whole conversation is voiced under one style direction. When the daily quota runs out, a browser-voice fallback keeps you working.
